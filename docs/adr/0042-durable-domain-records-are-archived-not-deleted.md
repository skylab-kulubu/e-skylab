# Durable domain records are archived, not physically deleted

Every active SKY LAB service keeps user-facing durable domain records by archiving, revoking, disabling, or anonymizing them instead of physically deleting their database row. The state name follows the domain (`archived_at`, `revoked_at`, `disabled_at`, `anonymized_at`) rather than forcing one generic flag; normal reads hide inactive records, authorized management reads can include and restore them when restoration is meaningful, and public delete operations are idempotent state transitions. Hard deletion remains required for secrets and ephemeral infrastructure data (sessions, one-time codes, reset tokens, caches, expired drafts and queue residue), relationship rows whose removal is itself the fact, and personal data erased during account deletion; soft deletion must not become a way to retain PII indefinitely. Inactive legacy repositories are not migrated—the policy applies to active production owners: core, Forms, CMS, SkyMail, Place, Keycloak, and the new Account center.
